Who We Are

Governance expertise, applied to privacy

As an ICSI-registered firm, our work has always spanned corporate secretarial and governance, capital markets and listing, M&A, insolvency, FEMA, accounting and audit, and ESG reporting — for clients ranging from listed companies and multinationals to mutual funds and early-stage startups.

Data protection is a natural extension of that governance work, not a bolt-on. A DPDP programme built by people who already think in terms of Board accountability and regulatory scrutiny tends to survive contact with an actual audit — which is the only kind of DPDP programme worth having.

Why Mehta & Mehta

Four Pillars That Set Us Apart

Governance DNA

We approach DPDP the way we approach every other Board-level obligation — as Company Secretaries and Chartered Accountants first, building a paper trail the Board can actually stand behind, not just a technical checklist.

Regulatory Overlay

Most of our clients already answer to RBI, SEBI or IRDAI. We layer DPDP on top of what you're already doing for those regulators, so the obligations line up instead of pulling in different directions.

Audit-Ready Output

We write every policy, register and notice assuming someone will eventually ask to see it — an auditor, the Board, or the Data Protection Board itself.

Single Point of Contact

One Senior Partner owns your engagement end to end, backed by a 150-plus-strong team of company secretaries, lawyers and accountants, and answers directly to our Founding Partners.
