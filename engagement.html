Engagement Model

A Structured, Four-Phase Approach

Engagements are structured across four phases. Clients may engage a single phase or the full lifecycle through to the May 2027 deadline.

Phase I

Discover & Diagnose

Applicability scoping, data discovery and mapping, and a risk-rated gap assessment with a Board-approved remediation roadmap.

Phase II

Design & Document

Notices, consent architecture, policies, vendor contracts, breach playbook and the full documentation suite.

Phase III

Implement & Embed

Rights and grievance workflows, DPIA, RoPA, security controls, vendor remediation and training ahead of the 13 May 2027 deadline.

Phase IV

Sustain & Assure

Annual DPIA and audit, breach support, regulatory watch, Board reporting and an annual health check.

Why It Works
