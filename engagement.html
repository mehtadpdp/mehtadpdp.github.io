Engagement Model

A Structured, Four-Phase Approach

Rather than one open-ended mandate, we split the work into four phases with a clear start and end. Bring us in for just the first — most clients do, at least initially — or carry it through to the May 2027 deadline and the compliance years that follow.

Phase I

Discover & Diagnose

Work out where the Act actually applies, find and map the data, and leave with a risk-rated gap assessment and a roadmap the Board has signed off on.

Phase II

Design & Document

Turn the diagnosis into the actual artefacts — notices, consent flows, policies, vendor contracts, a breach playbook, and everything else in the documentation set.

Phase III

Implement & Embed

Get the rights and grievance workflows, DPIA, RoPA, security controls and vendor fixes actually running — plus the training needed to go live before 13 May 2027.

Phase IV

Sustain & Assure

Keep it running: the annual DPIA and audit, breach support on call, regulatory watch, Board reporting, and a yearly health check to catch drift before it becomes a gap.

Why It Works

Engage One Phase, or the Full Lifecycle

Most engagements start small — a Phase I diagnostic gives the Board a clear, evidenced view of exposure before anyone commits to more. From there it's straightforward to scale into implementation and ongoing assurance, with the same Senior Partner accountable throughout.
